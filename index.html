About Me

Miranda Mittleman is a PhD candidate at the University of Michigan in the Robotics Department. She works under Dr. Leia Stirling in the Stirling Research Group. Her research is centered around wearable technology and human-robot interaction, with a focus in creating rehabilitation technologies to support older adults. She has previously gotten her M.S. in Robotics at UM, and completed her B.S. in Engineering Science at Trinity University in 2023. View her resume here.
